## 1. Introduction
Every pipeline should move a lead, client, or applicant from one stage to the next on its own, with a human stepping in only where the automation is deliberately designed to hand off to one. Automation handles the routine path; every pipeline names exactly where the human checkpoint is.

## 2. Complete GoHighLevel Pipeline Blueprint

### 2.1 Residential Pipeline
Moves a residential lead from enquiry to first booked and paid clean.
**Human checkpoint:** None required for standard bookings; CEO/VA reviews only flagged high-value or non-standard requests.
| Stage | Entry trigger | Automated actions | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Enquiry | Form submission, call to the GHL local number, website chatbot, or Local Services Ads click | Auto-reply within 60 seconds. Calls and chats answered live 24/7 by the AI Voice Agent/chatbot (Volume 4, Section 2.5a/2.5b; Volume 2, OPS-13) — name, email and mobile captured into this pipeline automatically. | Automation |
| Quoted | Property details declared (web form, chat, or call) | Published package price returned instantly from the ladder (Section 5.1). T&Cs acknowledgement captured and timestamped. | Automation |
| Booked | Customer accepts the price and selects a first-clean date | Confirmation SMS/email stating the price, the **2× first-visit charge**, and the bounded inclusions; pre-clean checklist (access/pets/parking) sent 48h and 2h before. | Automation |
| Payment Secured | Booking confirmed | Stripe pre-authorisation or payment link sent and confirmed before dispatch. | Automation |
| Job Complete | Crew marks job complete in Connecteam | Invoice/receipt issued; review request triggered (3Rs, Section 3.2); moved to Renewal pipeline (2.7) if recurring. | Automation |
| Non-Standard Request | Beyond 5 bed / 4 bath, condition rated High, or an Estate-level enquiry | **No instant price returned.** Routed to VA/CEO — Estate Care to the walkthrough path ($100, credited); High-condition properties to a quoted Deep Reset. | VA / CEO |
| Estate Walkthrough | Estate Care enquiry accepted | $100 walkthrough fee taken, credited against the first clean; Walkthrough Scope Record completed on site (Section 5.1a); custom quote issued from $2,000/visit. | CEO / Sales Manager |

**Two paths, cleanly separated.** Essential and Signature go New Enquiry → Quoted → Booked, with no human in the loop. Estate Care and anything flagged Non-Standard never receive an instant price and route to a person. The voice agent and chatbot quote from the **published ladder only** — never a derived, discounted or discretionary figure (Volume 4, Section 3).

### 2.6 Lost Opportunity Pipeline
Recovers value from leads who were quoted but did not proceed.
**Human checkpoint:** None — fully automated re-engagement, with any reply routed to a human.

### 2.7 Renewal Pipeline
Manages recurring plan continuity so no client relationship lapses through inattention, protecting the MRR metric.
**Human checkpoint:** Pricing/terms change sign-off (Sales Manager/CEO).

|---|---|---|---|

| Terms Confirmed | Review complete | Renewed plan (same or updated terms) confirmed. | Automation |
| Renewed | Confirmed | New renewal task created for the next cycle. | Automation |
| At Risk | Client indicates hesitation or requests changes | Escalated immediately to CEO/Sales Manager. | CEO / Sales Manager |
| Not Renewed | Client cancels | Moved to Win-Back pipeline (2.8); offboarding (OPS-09) triggered. | Automation |

### 2.8 Win-Back Pipeline
Re-engages clients who cancelled, on a cadence that respects their decision rather than pestering them.
**Human checkpoint:** None — fully automated, any reply routed to Sales Manager.
| Stage | Entry trigger | Automated actions | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Churned | Client cancels | Exit reason logged if known. | Automation |

## 3. Customer Onboarding Journey & Nurture Automations

### 3.1 Residential Onboarding Journey
Lighter-touch than a formal enterprise onboarding, optimised for speed from enquiry to first clean while still reinforcing the premium positioning (Volume 1, Section 5.3).
| Step | Timing | What happens | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instant quote | Immediately on enquiry | Published package price returned from declared property details (Section 5.1); T&Cs acknowledgement timestamped. | Web / SMS + email |
| Booking confirmation | On acceptance | Confirms date, time, package price, the 2× first-visit charge, and exactly what is and is not included. | Email |
| Booking confirmation | On booking | Confirms date, time, price, and what to expect. | Email |
| Pre-clean checklist | 48h and 2h before | Access instructions, pet/parking notes, anything to secure beforehand. | SMS |
| Post-clean review request | Immediately after job marked complete | 3Rs review request (Section 3.2). | SMS/email |
| Recurring plan offer | For one-off/acquisition cleans, 7 days after | Offers a recurring plan per the revenue conversion logic (Volume 1, Section 2.8). | Email |

### 3.2 Nurture Automations — Retention
- **3Rs review-generation program:** triggered after every completed job. **⚠️ Open item O4 — this mechanism needs restructuring before launch.** As previously written it asked a satisfaction question first and routed only positive responses to a public review request. That is review gating: selective solicitation of positive reviews is prohibited under Google's review policies and has drawn ACCC attention as potentially misleading conduct under the Australian Consumer Law. Vol 5 §5.3 compounds it by tying subcontractor incentives to verified Google reviews.
  **The intent is sound and worth keeping** — catching dissatisfaction early is good service recovery. The fix is to decouple the two paths: invite *every* completed job to review publicly, and run the satisfaction question as a **separate, internal** trigger that creates a service-recovery task on a low score. Detection stays; the filter on who gets asked goes. Confirm with Lawpath before go-live.
- **Pre-visit reminder:** SMS 24 hours and 2 hours before every recurring visit.
- **Between-visit value content:** monthly email/SMS touch with seasonal tips or service reminders — never purely promotional.
- **Anniversary/loyalty touch:** automated acknowledgement at each 12-month service anniversary, with a small loyalty gesture for long-standing clients.
- **Payment failure recovery:** automated retry and friendly follow-up before it becomes an overdue-invoice issue.
- **Satisfaction dip detection:** two consecutive below-threshold responses on the same account automatically creates an Operations Manager task.

## 4. Funnel Library

### 4.1 Segment-First Architecture
The website is built around four buyer-segment landing pages, not a service-type list. Competitor review (Zones 1–5) found that every reviewed competitor — including the closest positioning match, The Gold Standard Co. — mixes luxury residential, standard cleaning, and Airbnb turnover under one undifferentiated page and voice. Splitting by buyer segment is a deliberate point of difference: each segment is comparing Vantage Point against a different set of alternatives and needs different proof, a different CTA, and its own nurture track. Mixing them dilutes the price anchor (e.g. Estate Care content sitting next to a standard-clean price undercuts it) and risks sending the wrong lead the wrong follow-up.

**Launch phasing (Volume 1, Section 2.2a): only the Home Care page is live and marketed at launch.** Luxury Homes, Estates, and Turnover are built during the MVP phase but stay unpublished/unlinked until 20 customers hold an active recurring Home Care plan. Building all four now and launching one avoids a second build cycle later, while keeping crew training, sales messaging, and marketing spend concentrated on proving the Home Care assessment flow first.

> **Current site deviation:** `site/estate-care.html` is published and linked in the main navigation of all three live pages. That is the Estates segment page, which this gate defers to Phase 3. It should be unlinked until the gate clears.

**Cross-page rules:**
- No page links to another segment's pricing or tier language.
- Reviews/testimonials stay segment-specific — a Home Care review is not proof for an Estates prospect.
- All four pages share the same visual brand system; differentiation is in language, proof, and CTA only.
- Full page-by-page content brief (headline direction, proof points, objection handling, imagery notes) is maintained as a companion document to this volume.

This reverses the assessment-first decision taken earlier in August 2026 — Vol 1 §5.3 records what the reversal costs and what it requires.

**Because the price is issued before anyone visits, declared scope is the pricing basis.** The mandatory timestamped T&Cs acknowledgement at submission (Section 5.1) is the moment the variance clause becomes operative for that booking, and OPS-16 (Volume 2) governs the on-site conversation if the property materially differs from what was declared. This is the mechanism that makes instant quoting survivable without absorbing customer underestimation risk.

**The first visit is charged at 2× the package price**, disclosed at booking — a first clean runs two to three times an ongoing one. Properties whose condition is rated High route to a quoted Deep Reset rather than straight onto a plan.

## 5. Minimum Form Field Specifications

### 5.1 Instant Quote Form (Home Care & Luxury Homes — public web form)
Produces a binding package price from declared property details. Same field set drives the chatbot and voice agent, so a caller and a web visitor reach the same number.

**Required to produce a price:** Name; Phone; Email; Suburb/address; **Bedrooms**; **Bathrooms (full)**; **Powder rooms / separate toilets**; **Living/dining areas**; Service tier (Essential or Signature); Preferred frequency (weekly or fortnightly).

**Required to protect the price:** **Property condition** self-rating (Low / Medium / High — gates the first-visit decision); Multi-level (Y/N); Outdoor/alfresco (Y/N); Pool surrounds (Y/N); Balconies/terraces (count); Study (Y/N); Media room (Y/N); Walk-in robes (count); Garage to be serviced (Y/N).

**Optional:** Pets on site; Parking/access notes; Special requests; Photographs (encouraged — strongest protection against a later scope dispute).

**Consent:** Privacy Collection Notice at the point of submission (Section 5.8).

**T&Cs acknowledgement — required, timestamped, logged against the booking:**
> *"I confirm the details above are accurate and understand that Vantage Point Cleaning's Terms & Conditions apply if the property's actual size or condition differs materially from what I've declared."*

This checkbox is the moment the scope-variance clause becomes operative. It must block submission if unchecked.

**Routing.** Configurations beyond **5 bedrooms or 4 bathrooms**, or any property with condition rated **High**, do not receive an instant price — they route to a human as a Non-Standard Request (Section 2.1). Estate-level enquiries route to the walkthrough path.

### 5.1a Walkthrough Scope Record (Estate Care only — completed on site)
Not a web form. Completed during the Estate Care walkthrough, on mobile, attached to the CRM contact record, and used to build the custom quote. Full space-by-space capture: floor area, every room type and count, condition assessment, photographs, access arrangements.

Estate Care is the only tier priced this way. Essential and Signature are priced from the customer's declaration above.

### 5.2 Payment / Checkout Form
**Required:** Name matching booking; Card details (via Stripe-hosted fields — never captured directly); Billing email.
**Optional:** Billing address.
Card data always passes through Stripe's hosted fields; never stored in GoHighLevel or Xero directly, for PCI compliance.

### 5.3 Nurture / Communications Opt-In
**Required:** Name; Phone or email (at least one); Consent checkbox (SMS/email marketing, per Spam Act / Privacy Act requirements).
**Optional:** Preferred channel.
Consent is mandatory and logged with a timestamp.

### 5.4 Post-Clean Check-In / Satisfaction Survey
**Required:** Job/client reference (auto-populated); Satisfaction rating (1–5 or thumbs up/down).
**Optional:** Free-text comment.
Rating below threshold routes privately to Operations Manager; above threshold routes to the public review request.

### 5.5 Recruitment Application Form
**Required:** Full name; Phone; Email; Right to work in Australia (declaration); Suburb/location; Availability; Relevant experience (years).
**Optional:** Resume upload; References; Own vehicle/tools (Y/N).

### 5.6 Referral Submission Form
**Required:** Referrer name; Referrer contact (for reward tracking); Referred contact name; Referred contact phone or email.
**Optional:** Relationship/context; Preferred introduction method.

### 5.7 Supplier Onboarding Form
**Required:** Company name; ABN; Contact name; Contact details; Category; Certificate of Currency (if applicable).
**Optional:** Pricing/quote attachment; Payment terms requested.

### 5.8 Privacy Collection Notice — applies to every form above
**Open item — not yet drafted. Blocking for go-live.** Flagged in the August 2026 audit as C3/O9.

Australian Privacy Principle 5 requires that individuals be notified, at or before the point of collection, of who is collecting their information, why, who it may be disclosed to, and how to access or complain about it. Every form in this section collects personal information; several collect a great deal of it.

Required before any form goes live:
- A short collection notice rendered at the point of submission on every public form — not buried in a linked policy.
- A Privacy Policy published at a real URL. `site/index.html` currently links it to `href="#"`.
- A stated **retention period and deletion trigger** for property photographs and home access details (keys, codes, alarm information) under COM-06. Nothing in the suite currently states one.
- Marketing consent captured separately from service consent (Section 5.3) — consent to be contacted about a booking is not consent to be marketed to.

Draft via Lawpath alongside the Terms & Conditions (Volume 1, Section 3.9).

## 6. Recruitment Automation — Filtering & Onboarding

### 6.1 Automated Screening Logic
Applied instantly on Section 5.5 form submission:
- Right-to-work unchecked or "no" → automatically declined, polite templated message.
- Location outside the defined service zone (Volume 1, Section 2.7) → declined, or flagged for a zone-specific role.
- Availability doesn't overlap current roster gaps → held in a talent pool.
- All criteria passed → automatically advanced to "Screened," reference/background check (including police check) triggered.

Screening declines only on objective, job-relevant criteria — right to work, location, availability — never on any protected attribute, consistent with the Fair Work Act and Anti-Discrimination Act.

### 6.2 Interview & Offer Handoff
- Self-service booking link for a screening call sent automatically.
- Interview questions pre-loaded from a standard question bank.
- Pass triggers offer/contract send; decline triggers the same polite templated close.

### 6.3 Onboarding Automation
Once an offer is accepted, parallel tasks are created:
- Finance Lead: payroll setup, due before the first rostered shift.
- HR Lead: handbook distribution, system access provisioning.
- Cleaning Supervisor: field induction and first-shift shadowing.
- Every task carries a due date tied to the start date; overdue tasks escalate automatically to the CEO.
