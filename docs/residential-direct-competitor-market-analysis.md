## Positioning Framework
Every competitor is plotted on two axes:

- **Price orientation:** *Value* (competes on affordability/instant online pricing) vs. **Premium** (competes on trust, presentation, discretion; price not the lead message).
- **Service structure:** *Standard* (generic residential cleaning, often bundled with bond/Airbnb/commercial) vs. **Specialised/Premium** (single-service focus, recurring-relationship model, or explicit luxury/estate targeting).

A competitor can be Premium-priced but Standard-structured (markets "premium" language while still bundling every service type under one roof) — this combination is in fact the **most common pattern found in this market**, and it's the specific gap Vantage Point's segment-first, single-brand-focus model is built to exploit.

**Vantage Point's target quadrant:** Premium price / Specialised structure — currently occupied by only three competitors across all five zones combined, none of whom cover the full luxury-through-estate spectrum with a documented, verifiable quality system.

## 4. Market-Wide Patterns (For Strategy Reference)
- **The single biggest gap across all 23 competitors profiled:** none publish or reference any third-party-verifiable quality/inspection data. Every "premium" or "quality" claim in this market is presentation- and testimonial-based. This remains Vantage Point's clearest, most defensible point of difference.
- **National franchises (Absolute Domestics, Simply Maid) are present in every zone** but compete on brand trust and convenience/instant pricing — not relationship or premium positioning.
- **Short-stay/Airbnb turnover is bundled into most competitors' core offer** (Gold Coast Cleans, Whole Detail, Calibre, Steam Boss) — Vantage Point's decision to treat turnover as a selective, property-manager-only line is a structural point of difference, not just a messaging one.
- **Only three competitors sit in the Premium-price/Specialised-structure quadrant** Vantage Point is targeting: The Gold Standard Co., Bloom Clean, Land Based Cleaning. Everything else is either priced differently or structured differently (or both).
- **No competitor found runs a segment-split website/funnel structure** (separate pages for standard home care vs. luxury vs. estate vs. turnover) — every competitor, including the two highest-threat ones, markets all their services from a single undifferentiated page or brand voice.
