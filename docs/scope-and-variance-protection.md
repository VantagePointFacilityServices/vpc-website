# Vantage Point Cleaning — Scope & Variance Protection
Companion to Residential Volume 1 (Pricing), Volume 2 (SOPs), and Volume 3 (Forms/Funnels)

> **Rewritten twice in August 2026.** The audit removed the instant-quote model and rescoped this document to *condition* variance. Instant quoting was then reinstated for Essential and Signature (`residential-package-and-pricing-model.md`), so this document has reverted to its original purpose: protecting a price issued from **customer-declared scope**, before anyone has seen the property.

---

## 1. The Mechanism, Restated

Customer declares property scope on the quote form → published package price is returned instantly from the ladder → if the crew finds on arrival that actual scope materially exceeds the declaration, the customer chooses between two pre-agreed paths before extra work proceeds:

- **Option A — Extend:** additional time billed at **$100/hour** above the package's allocated hours, approved before it starts.
- **Option B — Stay fixed:** package price and time cap hold; work is completed in a pre-agreed priority order; anything not reached within the cap is left undone, as agreed.

**This is what makes instant quoting viable.** Essential and Signature are priced from a declaration the business cannot verify in advance. Without this mechanism, every underestimate — honest or otherwise — is absorbed by the business or by the cleaner.

**Applies to Essential and Signature only.** Estate Care is walkthrough-quoted and therefore physically verified before a price exists; OPS-16 does not apply to it.

**Not a licence to upcharge.** The threshold is objective, the conversation happens before extra work starts, and the customer always has a no-extra-cost option. If the SOP fires often, the quote form is asking the wrong questions — fix the form, not the invoice.

**Note the rate relationship.** The $100/hour variance rate sits above the $130/hour pricing basis on a per-labour-hour view only because variance work is unplanned, single-visit, and cannot be routed for density. Against the $50/hour cleaner rate it yields a 2× multiple — the same floor the packages are built on.

## 2. Draft Terms & Conditions Clause — Property Scope & Fixed-Price Variance
**Status: working draft for Lawpath review — not legal advice, and not yet a binding document.** Volume 1 §3.9 lists Lawpath-drafted Terms & Conditions as a required legal foundation; this clause belongs inside that document, not as a standalone side-agreement.

> **Property Scope Declaration.** The price and estimated duration of this service are calculated entirely from the property details you provide at booking — bedrooms, bathrooms, powder rooms, living and dining areas, and any additional spaces you select. You warrant that this information is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge at the time of booking.
>
> **On-Site Variance.** If, on arrival, our team reasonably determines that the property's actual size, room count or condition materially exceeds what was declared — such that the service cannot reasonably be completed to our standard within the allocated time — we will contact you before proceeding further, and document the variance (photographs and/or measurements) for our records.
>
> **Your Options.** Where a material variance arises, you may choose:
> (a) **Extend the service** at an additional rate of $100 per hour (or part thereof, billed in 30-minute increments) above the allocated time, payable in addition to your plan price; or
> (b) **Keep your plan price and time allocation.** We will complete the work in the priority order agreed with you at that time (or, where pre-agreed, the standard priority order for your service tier), and any remaining areas or tasks not completed within the allocated time will not be completed as part of that service, at no additional charge and with no discount against your plan price.
>
> **No Response.** If we are unable to reach you within approximately 15 minutes of attempting contact by phone, SMS and email, we will default to Option (b) so as not to delay our team's schedule, unless you have pre-authorised Option (a) in writing.
>
> **Repeat Variance.** Where the same property shows a material variance from its declared scope on a second occasion, we reserve the right to require an in-person or video property assessment before confirming any further instant-quote bookings for that address.

**Notes for Lawpath review:**
- **"Materially exceeds" carries a defined threshold** so crews are not making a subjective call on site: actual floor area more than 10–15% above declared, one or more entirely undeclared rooms or serviceable spaces, or a property booked at Low/Medium condition that plainly requires a reset. Getting this wrong in either direction is costly: too loose and it reads as an upcharge mechanism, too tight and crews absorb unpaid work.
- The $100/hour rate sits mid-band in Volume 1 §4.4's High-ticket/Premium hourly benchmark ($75–120+/hr) and well above the fully loaded labour cost, giving roughly 50–55% margin on the overage. That is appropriately higher than the standard job margin, since this is unplanned, on-demand extension work rather than scheduled capacity.
- The 15-minute no-response default carries a customer-experience risk — work left undone without the customer realising contact was attempted. The SMS + call + email sequence inside that window should be specified explicitly in the SOP (§4), not only in the T&Cs.
- **A second variance at the same address should end instant quoting for that address**, not repeat the conversation. Move it to a walkthrough-confirmed price.

## 3. Where Property Capture Happens

**Essential and Signature** — the customer declares scope on the public quote form (Volume 3, §5.1): bedrooms, bathrooms, powder rooms, living areas, plus the increment questions and a condition self-rating. That declaration is the pricing basis and the evidentiary record.

**Estate Care** — the assessor completes the Walkthrough Scope Record on site (Volume 3, §5.1a). Physically verified, so no variance mechanism is needed.

**The T&Cs acknowledgement sits at form submission** — mandatory, timestamped, blocking. It is the moment the clause above becomes operative for that booking, and it is the single most important field on the form. A quote issued without it is unenforceable.

**Photographs are encouraged but optional**, and remain the strongest protection against a later dispute. Consider a small incentive for photo-verified bookings.

## 4. SOP — On-Site Scope Variance Verification
Full text in Volume 2, Section 5 (OPS-16). Summary:

**SOP number:** OPS-16 · **Owner:** Cleaning Supervisor (crew lead, or CEO, pre-hire) · **Related:** OPS-02, QUA-01, FIN-01, COM (T&Cs clause above)

1. On arrival, the crew lead does a two-minute walkthrough against the declared property details on the booking, before starting work.
2. If actual scope materially exceeds the declaration — more than 10–15% above declared floor area, one or more entirely undeclared rooms, or condition plainly requiring a reset — photograph the discrepancy and log it in Connecteam against the job.
3. Contact the customer — phone, then SMS, then email — explaining the variance in plain terms and presenting the two options.
4. Confirm the customer's choice in writing before work proceeds past the point of variance.
5. If unreachable after all three attempts within ~15 minutes, default to Option (b) and log the attempted-contact timestamps.
6. Option A: track extension time from the agreed start, bill in 30-minute increments, show as a separate invoice line item (FIN-01).
7. Option B: work the pre-agreed priority order and note uncompleted areas on the job completion report shared with the customer.
8. Log every instance for trend review. A second material variance at the same address ends instant quoting for that address.

**Watch the rate.** A rising variance rate means the quote form is under-capturing, not that customers are dishonest. Feed every instance back into the Volume 3 §5.1 field list. Treat it as a form-design signal, never as a revenue line.

## 5. Quoting Models Across the Four Segments

| Segment | Quoting model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Home Care** (Essential/Signature) | Instant, from the published ladder. Priced from customer-declared scope; binding for that scope, subject to the variance clause above. | Speed and certainty at the point of enquiry. The variance mechanism, not an assessment, is what carries the underestimation risk. |
| **Luxury Homes** | Same ladder, Signature-tier framing. | Same mechanism; the differentiation is language and proof, not pricing model. |
| **Estates** | Consultation-first. No pricing bands published; the private consultation produces both the estimate and the confirmed price. | At this ticket size a walkthrough is the expected buying experience, not friction to remove. Unchanged from v1.0. |
| **Turnover (property managers)** | Contracted with the property manager, not priced per booking. OPS-16 does not apply. | Unchanged from v1.0. |

**Two mechanisms, cleanly split.** Essential and Signature quote instantly from declaration and rely on OPS-16. Estate Care sees the home first and needs no variance clause. Turnover is contracted. The split is by *tier*, not by segment.
