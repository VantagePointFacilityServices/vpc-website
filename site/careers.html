Pay & Conditions

The basics, done correctly

Nothing here is a perk. It is what you are entitled to, written down so you can hold us to it.

Paid properly, paid on time

Cleaning roles are covered by the Cleaning Services Award 2020 (MA000022). Your offer letter states your classification level and employment type, and you receive the Fair Work Information Statement at or before your start date.

Ways to earn above base

Ordinary hours, rostering and penalty rates follow the current Award rate table, and there are structured incentive programs on top of base rate.

Leave handled by the book

Part-time and full-time employees accrue leave under the Fair Work National Employment Standards. Casuals are paid casual loading in lieu.

Uniform and PPE provided

Uniform and personal protective equipment are issued at induction and replaced when worn — not something you buy yourself.

Training & Support

You are not sent out to work it out alone

Consistency for our clients starts with people who were actually shown how the work is done here.

A 30-day induction

Onboarding runs from the moment you accept the offer through your first 30 days, with a structured Day One, first week and first month — not a uniform and a set of keys.

A buddy from day one

New starters are paired with an experienced team member rather than sent out alone to work it out.

Training that is actually delivered

Core modules cover WHS and chemical handling, the inspection standard, the cleaning protocol for each service tier, emergency response, manual handling and in-home conduct — in person and shadowed, with set refresher cadences.

Somewhere to raise a concern

Your Cleaning Supervisor is the first port of call. For anything involving your supervisor, you go directly to the CEO or HR Lead through a documented grievance process.

Where You Work

Close to home, not all over the coast

We work five tight zones from Burleigh through to Sanctuary Cove rather than spreading across the whole Gold Coast. Less time driving between jobs means a schedule that holds and a supervisor who can actually reach you. See the zones.

Interested in joining us?

We are not always advertising a specific role, but we would rather hear from good people early. Send us a note and we will keep you in mind.
