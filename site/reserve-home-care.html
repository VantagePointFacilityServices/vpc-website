Reserve Home Care FAQ

Reserve includes everything in Signature, plus two rotational deep-clean tasks per visit instead of one (the eight-task cycle completes in four visits), up to two laundry loads instead of one, detailed bathrooms on every visit, and a priority reschedule guarantee with a replacement slot within 48 hours.

Reserve is a flat price per visit set by the size of your home. It starts from $569 for a studio or one-bedroom home, and a 3 bed / 2 bath home is $829 per visit (GST included, weekday visits). Your exact price, including any extras such as a pool or study, comes from your instant quote. Your first visit is priced at twice the package price because it brings the home to our standard, and this is shown before you book. Compare all three packages.
