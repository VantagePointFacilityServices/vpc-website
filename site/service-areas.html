Why It Matters

Density is the service standard

The same team

Short distances between homes mean one team can hold a region — so you see the same faces, not whoever was free.

Arrival windows we can keep

Less time on the motorway means the schedule holds, and a late run on one home doesn't cascade through the day.

Supervisors who can actually attend

A supervisor can reach any home in a region quickly, so quality checks happen in person rather than on paper.

One price, coast-wide

Every region is priced identically per visit. There is no travel loading and no premium for being further out.
