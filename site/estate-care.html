Who It's For

Not a house cleaner — a private estate manager

Estate Care is built for owners of larger, high-value properties who compare us against private estate managers and concierge property services, not weekly cleaners. Most alternatives in this category compete on breadth of staff, not consistency of care — a different cleaner or supervisor every time is the real cost most owners quietly absorb.

  • One dedicated team who come to know your home
  • A detailed, rotational maintenance program — not a single annual deep clean
  • Direct Supervisor inspection on every visit
  • Concierge-style communication with one point of contact
