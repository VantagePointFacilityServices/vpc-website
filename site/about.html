How we work

Why we run on systems, not memory

By day, Blake is a software engineer. Good software teams don’t rely on memory. They write things down, check their work and keep a record. Blake brought that habit to cleaning.

He doesn’t do the cleaning himself. His job is making sure it’s done right, every time. So every package has a written checklist, every home has its own notes, and every visit ends with before-and-after photos. The standard doesn’t depend on who’s rostered that week, or on anyone remembering.

Our standard

What “done properly” means to us

For Blake, being professional isn’t a polo shirt and a logo. It’s turning up on your day, cleaning what’s in your package, showing you the photos, and owning it when something’s missed.

That’s why he started with the boring parts most cleaning companies skip. Before the first clean, he wrote down how every routine job is done: how a home is entered and locked up, how keys and codes are kept private, how products are measured, and how a cleaner behaves in your home. New cleaners learn from those procedures, not from whoever trained them last.

It also means being honest about what we are. We’re a young, local business, and we’d rather show you how we work than make big claims. If something isn’t up to standard, we fix the process so it doesn’t happen again.

What we stand for

Four things we won’t trade away

We want relationships where clients, cleaners and their families all do well, and enjoy the Gold Coast lifestyle.

Value over price

We won’t be the cheapest quote, and we’ll tell you that up front. A cleaner who cuts corners usually costs more in the end.

Data over promises

Anyone can promise a great clean on the phone. We’d rather show you: a written checklist for your package, and before-and-after photos from every visit.

Systems compound

Every repeatable job gets written down. When something works, it becomes the standard. When something slips, we fix the process, not just the problem.

Compliant by default

Fair pay, safe work and your privacy are built in from day one, not bolted on after a complaint.
