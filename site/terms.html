4. Property Scope Declaration and On-Site Variance

Property Scope Declaration. The price and estimated duration of this service are calculated entirely from the property details you provide at booking — bedrooms, bathrooms, powder rooms, living and dining areas, and any additional spaces you select. You warrant that this information is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge at the time of booking.

On-Site Variance. If, on arrival, our team reasonably determines that the property's actual size, room count or condition materially exceeds what was declared — such that the service cannot reasonably be completed to our standard within the allocated time — we will contact you before proceeding further, and document the variance (photographs and/or measurements) for our records.

Your Options. Where a material variance arises, you may choose: (a) Extend the service at an additional rate of $100 per hour (or part thereof, billed in 30-minute increments) above the allocated time, payable in addition to your plan price; or (b) Keep your plan price and time allocation. We will complete the work in the priority order agreed with you at that time (or, where pre-agreed, the standard priority order for your service tier), and any remaining areas or tasks not completed within the allocated time will not be completed as part of that service, at no additional charge and with no discount against your plan price.

No Response. If we are unable to reach you within approximately 15 minutes of attempting contact by phone, SMS and email, we will default to Option (b) so as not to delay our team's schedule, unless you have pre-authorised Option (a) in writing.

Repeat Variance. Where the same property shows a material variance from its declared scope on a second occasion, we reserve the right to require an in-person or video property assessment before confirming any further instant-quote bookings for that address.

5. Payment

  • You authorise us to charge the card you saved at booking for:
    • each visit, after the visit is completed;
    • any late-cancellation charge under clause 6;
    • any extension you choose under clause 4(a).
  • Each charge is raised as an invoice and charged automatically; you'll receive the invoice by email.
  • If a charge fails, we'll contact you straight away to update your card. If it's still unpaid 7 days after it failed, we'll pause your upcoming visits until it's paid.

6. Late Cancellation and Rescheduling

Late Cancellation & Rescheduling. If you cancel or reschedule a booked visit with less than 24 hours' notice before its scheduled start time, we will charge 50% of that visit's price to your saved payment method, to cover the wages already committed to the cleaner rostered for your visit. For a first visit, this 50% is calculated against the first-visit price (double the ongoing package price, since a first visit takes longer and is staffed accordingly) — not against the ongoing package price. This clause applies to every visit under an Essential, Signature or Reserve package, and to every visit under a signed Estate Care contract.

How it's charged. The 50% late-cancellation charge is raised as its own invoice against the same saved card used for your booking, charged automatically at the time of the late cancellation or reschedule — it is not deducted from a future visit's charge.

What counts as 24 hours. Measured from the moment you notify us — by phone, SMS, email, or through our booking system — to the scheduled start time of the visit being cancelled or rescheduled.

Lockout. If our team arrives at the scheduled time and can't get in (for example no key, a wrong access code, or nobody home when you said someone would be), we'll try to contact you. If we still can't get in, we'll charge 50% of that visit's price (of the first-visit price for a first visit), the same as a late cancellation, because the cleaner's time was already committed. Our team records the attempt with a timestamped check-in and a photo.

7. Access, keys and safety

  • Tell us how we'll get in (key, lockbox, someone home) through the pre-clean checklist. Don't put door or alarm codes in the online quote form; we collect access details separately and store them securely.
  • Please secure pets, valuables and anything fragile, and tell us about any hazards before the visit.
  • Our team may decline to clean an area that's unsafe, and will tell you if they do.

8. Ending or pausing your package

There's no lock-in. You can cancel your package at any time, with no fee, by telling us at least 48 hours before your next scheduled visit. You can cancel by email or phone, and we'll confirm in writing. If you cancel less than 48 hours before the next visit, that visit goes ahead as booked, or the late-cancellation rule in clause 6 applies if you cancel it too.

We may stop providing services if you don't pay, if our team is unsafe at the property, or if the repeat-variance rule in clause 4 applies. We'll tell you in writing.
