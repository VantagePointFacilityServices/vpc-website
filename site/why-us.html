How it works

How we get started

  1. Tell us about your home. Bedrooms, bathrooms, how often you’d like us, and which package suits you: Essential, Signature or Reserve.
  2. You get it in writing. Your price per visit, and what’s included, before you commit to anything.
  3. We write up your home notes. Before the first visit we confirm access, pets, anything fragile or off limits, and what matters most to you. Your cleaner reads them before they arrive, so nothing is left to guesswork on the day.
  4. Your first visit resets the home. It brings everything up to the standard we’ll then keep. It takes longer, so it’s priced at twice a regular visit, and we tell you that up front.
  5. We check in after your first clean. We’ll ask how it went and sort out anything that isn’t right before we settle into the routine.
  6. Then the same cleaner wherever possible, every visit. Same checklist, same photos, on your weekly or fortnightly day.

Proof of work

What happens on every clean

Here’s what goes on behind the scenes, so you know what you’re paying for.

Checked before they set foot in your home

Every cleaner is police-checked before their first shift, employed by us and trained on our written standard. Nobody is sent to your home who hasn’t been through that.

Your keys and codes stay private

Whether you leave a key, use a lockbox or are home, we agree access before the first visit. Keys and codes are never copied, shared or photographed, and we never ask for alarm or door codes through an online form.

The right product, used properly

Products are measured to the label, and there’s a Safety Data Sheet on file for every one we use. Prefer we use something of yours on a surface? Tell us and we’ll check it first.

Every visit opens and closes the same way

Your cleaner takes before photos, works through your package checklist, ticks it off, takes after photos and leaves a note. That record is what our guarantee and your peace of mind are built on.

We’re careful with what we photograph

We only photograph the work. Never people, documents or screens. Photos are used to show you the job and to sort out any question about it, and nothing else.

Your home, treated like a home

Your cleaner arrives in uniform, on your agreed day, and treats your home with the respect you would. If something’s fragile or off limits, it stays that way.

Straight answers

Straight answers

Am I locked in?

No. Cancel any time, with no fee, by giving us at least 48 hours’ notice before your next visit.

Will I get a regular cleaner?

The same cleaner wherever possible. If they’re ever away, a briefed replacement covers the visit and we’ll tell you who’s coming before they arrive.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients aren’t. We agree access before your first visit, whether that’s a key, a lockbox or someone at home.

What about my pets?

Pets are welcome. We just ask that they’re settled or secured while we clean, and that you let us know about them beforehand.

What if something’s missed?

Tell us within 48 hours and we’ll come back within 3 days to fix it, at no charge. That’s our guarantee.

What if I need to reschedule?

Give us at least 48 hours’ notice and there’s no charge. Late changes are charged at half the visit, because your cleaner’s time was set aside for you.

How do I get ready for a clean?

Put away valuables and anything fragile, settle the pets, and tell us about anything we should know, like a broken tile or a room to skip. You don’t need to tidy up for us.

What will it cost?

A flat price per visit for your package, in writing before you book. See the packages or get in touch for your price.
